Updated 31 October 2026

Our approach

We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design and improvement target. The site has not undergone a complete independent accessibility audit, and we do not claim full conformance or publish an Accessibility Conformance Report or Declaration of Conformance.

Using the website

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move through controls and Enter or Space to activate them. Post dialogs can be closed with Escape or their Close button. On wide screens the post and comments appear beside each other; on smaller screens they stack. Search results are keyboard links. Your operating system’s reduced-motion preference is respected by the community animations.

Known limitations

Community files may be inaccessible. Descriptions are required for new image and video attachments, but their accuracy depends on contributors. Uploaded videos may lack captions or transcripts; a description is not a replacement for captions. Uploaded PDFs, text files and archives have not been accessibility-audited. Discord sign-in and other third-party services have their own accessibility behaviour. Full screen-reader, magnification and cross-browser testing is still pending.

Report a barrier

Email gta6onlinediscord@gmail.com or contact our Discord support server. Describe the page, what you were trying to do, and optionally your browser or assistive technology. Do not include passwords or sensitive account details. We will review the issue and discuss an accessible alternative where possible.
