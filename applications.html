Recruitment privacy notice

Version recruitment-2026-11-01 · Effective 1 November 2026

Operated by unnamed, Muscat, Oman. Applications are private and available to authorized website staff for assessing moderator suitability and contacting you through Discord. We collect your website account reference, Discord username, age in years, timezone, availability, answers and optional experience details, plus submission time and this notice version. Do not include passwords, identity documents, home addresses or other people’s private details.

Submitting is voluntary and does not guarantee a staff role. Applications are stored using our existing Supabase service in Germany. They become inaccessible after 90 days and are automatically deleted by a cleanup job running every minute. Provider backups, if present, follow the provider’s separate retention schedule. We do not publish applications, use them for advertising, or sell them.

You must be 16 or older. Applicants aged 16–17 and anyone needing guardian consent must have a verified guardian explicitly approve this recruitment processing and overseas storage before providing answers. General account approval alone is not enough. Contact the operator through our Discord support server for verification, access, correction, withdrawal or deletion. You can also withdraw your submitted application here. Your answers are not saved as a browser draft.

Read the Terms, Privacy Policy and Community Rules. No new advertising or analytics cookies are used.
