What this site uses

We do not deploy analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or marketing trackers. This website uses a small browser storage value, not a tracking cookie, solely to remember whether you acknowledged our storage notice. No optional tracker is enabled by clicking “Got it.”

Browser storage details

The value is named gta6online-cookie-notice-acknowledged and is stored in your browser’s local storage with the value yes. It stays on your device unless you clear it. It is not transmitted to our server through JavaScript.

Site hosting

Our static site is designed for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical requests and related logs when serving files; this notice does not govern GitHub’s own operations or its privacy practices.

Manage this preference

You can clear this site’s browser storage through your browser settings. You can also use the control below to remove the notice preference, which will make the notice appear again.
