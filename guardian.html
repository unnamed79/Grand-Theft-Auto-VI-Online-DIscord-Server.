ACCOUNT / GUARDIAN APPROVAL

A place to belong.
With your guidance.

For parents and legal guardians of members who meet Discord’s minimum age but need adult approval for a website account.

Process version 2026-10-31.3

Before Discord sign-in

If you are under 18 or need a legal representative, ask your guardian to read this page before you sign in. You can continue browsing public pages. The website does not collect identity documents or the guardian’s information through an online application.

Discord’s minimum age for the member’s country still applies. Guardian permission cannot override that minimum.

1. The guardian contacts the operator

The parent or legal guardian should email gta6onlinediscord@gmail.com with a request to discuss guardian approval. Initially, provide only your own contact details. Do not include the child’s identity documents, date of birth or Discord ID in the initial enquiry.

The operator is the individual publicly known as unnamed, based in Muscat, Oman. Contact information in an enquiry is used to respond and arrange verification.

2. Review the information and complete verification

The operator will explain the proposed verification method, what information is necessary and how it will be protected. Approval requires verification of the adult’s identity and parental or legal authority. A checkbox, a message from the child, or an unverified email address alone is not enough. Do not send passports, identity cards or birth certificates unless a suitable secure process has first been agreed.

Read the Terms of Service, Community Rules, Privacy Policy and Storage Policy. The guardian must separately approve account processing, publication of reviews and the described overseas processing. Signing in does not authorise payment or a Patreon subscription.

Discord and Supabase supply login and account services. Account data may include Discord identifiers, display name, avatar and email. The website does not receive Discord passwords. Reviews, star ratings and display names are public and may be copied by other visitors. Staff may moderate reviews. The Supabase project is hosted in Germany, and other providers may process information outside Oman.

3. Receive a private approval code

Only after verification and explicit guardian approval does the operator record the child’s Discord ID, supplied by the guardian, and issue a code for that account and policy version. Codes expire after seven days; contact the operator if a replacement is needed. Do not post or share a code publicly.

The member chooses the guardian route in the sign-in form, enters the code and reviews the policies. After Discord login, the website checks that the signed-in account matches the recorded approval before allowing a review or edit. A new policy version may require renewed guardian approval.

Your continuing choices

A guardian can ask the operator to access, correct or delete the child’s data, or withdraw approval. Once revocation is recorded, new reviews and edits are blocked. Existing reviews remain public until removed; request their removal when withdrawing if that is your preference. Members can also withdraw account consent in their profile menu and delete their own reviews. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing.

If verification cannot be completed, an approval code will not be issued. Public browsing remains available.
