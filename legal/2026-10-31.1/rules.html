Community / The rulebook

Good vibes.
Shared standards.

These rules apply across our community: conversations, reviews, posts, profiles and shared creations.

← Back to Community

Effective: 31 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-31.1

Rule 01

English only

Please communicate in English at all times.

Rule 02

No politics, religious debates or public drama

Avoid political, religious and controversial discussions that may cause conflict. Do not start or continue personal disputes or public callouts. Resolve issues privately or contact staff.

Rule 03

No NSFW or NSFL content

Sexually explicit, inappropriate or graphic real-world injury content is forbidden. If you are unsure whether something is appropriate, do not post it or participate in the discussion.

Rule 04

Be respectful

Keep conversations mature and respectful. Excessive profanity, hate speech, harassment, spam, toxicity and aggressive behavior are not allowed.

Rule 05

Use spoiler tags responsibly

Hide spoilers using the platform’s spoiler controls and make the topic clear. Do not misuse spoiler tags to disguise prohibited content. If a posting area has no spoiler controls, avoid posting the spoiler there.

Rule 06

Appropriate nicknames

Choose a respectful nickname. Offensive names, banned words and disrespectful profile names are not allowed.

Rule 07

No impersonation

Do not impersonate other members, bots, staff or public figures.

Rule 08

No advertising, begging or trading

Advertising, begging and trading are prohibited except permitted in-game GTA content or services, such as helping with sales or heists. This exception never permits scams or violations of Rockstar’s rules. Rockstar-related fan promotion is covered below.

Rule 09

No piracy, leaks or malicious links

Do not discuss or share piracy, illegal material or related links. NDA-protected leaks—including GTA VI leaked images, videos, documents and links—are forbidden. This includes AI-generated content based on leaks and fan art using leaked material. Phishing, scams, fake giveaways and malicious links are prohibited.

Rule 10

Rockstar-related promotion only

Self-promotion must directly relate to Rockstar Games. Fan creations, community maps, game modes and Rockstar-related YouTube videos are welcome when they are not spammed or misleading. Unrelated promotion is not allowed. All other community rules still apply.

Rule 11

Respect Rockstar’s rules

Do not share modded accounts, hacks, money drops, mod menus or other content that violates Rockstar’s rules. Read Rockstar’s community guidelines.

Keep the community welcoming

See something wrong?

Contact staff privately to report a concern or appeal a moderation decision. Honest criticism is welcome; harassment is not.

Staff may edit or remove community content and restrict access to enforce these rules. Moderation permissions do not grant general access to private account information.
