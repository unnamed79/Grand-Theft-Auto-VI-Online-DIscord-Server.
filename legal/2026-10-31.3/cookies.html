Privacy / Browser storage

Storage, explained.

Essential preferences and sign-in information.

Effective: 31 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-31.3

What the website stores

The website saves your dismissal of the storage notice. Across the website, Supabase uses browser storage for the authentication session and temporary information needed to complete Discord login. Sessions allow you to stay signed in and manage your reviews and community activity. During sign-in, temporary session storage holds the agreement choices and, for the guardian route, the private approval code. The pending record is consumed after the login return, rejected after 20 minutes, or removed on cancellation. The browser also remembers a dismissed agreement prompt within the current tab session and temporarily stores the website page to return to after login. The return-page record is consumed on a successful login return and only honoured for 20 minutes. Clearing this storage does not erase the agreement recorded on the server.

Your choices

You can read reviews and community posts without signing in. Use Sign out in your header profile menu to end your session, or clear this website’s browser data. Clearing browser storage does not delete your account or published reviews, posts, comments or votes. Request account deletion through our Discord support server.

Providers

Discord, Supabase and the library CDN may process technical information to provide their services. We have not added advertising or analytics trackers. Read our Privacy Policy for details of account and community information.
