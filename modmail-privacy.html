Scope and operator

This policy explains how GTA VI Online Community handles information through ModMail#0875 (application ID 1564972481557831790). It covers the ModMail Bot; Discord separately handles information under its Privacy Policy. Contact us through our Discord support server.

Information handled

The Bot processes Discord user IDs, usernames and avatars needed to identify participants; ticket messages, replies, attachments and timestamps; and server IDs and configuration such as staff roles and ticket or log channels. Only content associated with ModMail interactions is described here.

Who can read your messages

Sending a DM to the Bot sends its content to the server's designated staff. Staff replies may be relayed back to you, and ticket transcripts may be available to authorized staff. Do not treat ModMail as a private conversation with a single person. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Why information is used

Information is used to relay messages, manage tickets, answer support requests, maintain moderation records, prevent abuse, and resolve technical problems. We do not sell or rent this information or use it for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning or AI models.

Storage, security and retention

Ticket records and transcripts are retained indefinitely for moderation history unless a deletion request is made. This retention policy may change; any change will be reflected here. Stored bot data is not encrypted at rest. Removing the Bot or closing a ticket does not automatically delete existing records. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Access and service providers

Authorized server staff access tickets for support and moderation. The Bot's operators may access information needed for maintenance, security and support. Discord processes relayed messages on its platform. Technical service providers may process information needed to operate the Bot. Contact the team for information about the providers used for a particular ticket. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. We do not sell, rent or trade ticket data.

Choices and deletion requests

You can stop messaging the Bot, and administrators can remove it from a server. To request access, correction or deletion of information, join our Discord support server and contact staff. Provide your Discord user ID and enough context to locate the ticket; never send passwords or tokens. We may verify identity before acting. We manually verify requests and complete deletion of records under our control within 48–72 hours after verification. Contact staff to discuss any copies of your ticket, including transcripts or backups, as part of your request. Records held independently by Discord or exported by server administrators may require a separate request to those parties. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location.

Age requirements

The Bot is intended for users who are at least 13 and meet Discord's minimum age requirement in their country. Contact staff if you believe an underage person has submitted information, so the team can investigate and address it.

Policy updates

We will update the effective date when this policy changes and announce material changes through our support server. For questions or privacy requests, use our Discord support server. Read the ModMail Terms of Service.
