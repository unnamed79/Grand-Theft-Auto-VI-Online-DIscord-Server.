Overview

The website operator and privacy contact is the individual publicly known as unnamed, based in Muscat, Oman. Contact gta6onlinediscord@gmail.com for privacy requests, including guardian requests. The community name and public alias do not represent a registered company.

This policy concerns the GTA VI Online Community website and custom Discord bots operated by the community. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to gta6onlinediscord@gmail.com. Discord separately processes information under its own privacy policy.

Website accounts and community content

You can browse without signing in. Discord and Supabase provide sign-in for website reviews. We do not collect your Discord password. Supabase stores account and identity information supplied through authentication, which may include Discord identifiers, name, avatar and email. Authentication information is not included in the public reviews feed.

Your review, star rating, display name, timestamps, staff-edit label and an opaque website author identifier are public. Other visitors can copy public content. Do not post private information. We use account identifiers to enforce ownership and staff permissions, and store the time of your latest write to limit spam.

GitHub hosts the website; Supabase handles authentication and the community database and attachment storage; Discord provides the identity provider. The website loads a fixed version of the Supabase library through jsDelivr, which receives ordinary request information such as IP address. These providers handle data under their own policies.

Reviews remain until you delete them, staff remove them, or the account is deleted. Account records and submission-limit records remain while the account exists. Private moderation records contain action, actor identifier, review reference and time, not copies of review text. They are retained for moderation accountability and reviewed when deletion is requested. Provider logs and backups have their own retention; removing a live record does not promise immediate erasure from all provider backups.

Staff moderation permissions cover community content, not passwords or general access to private account records. Website operators with backend administration access can access information needed for security, operations and privacy requests. We do not sell account information or use reviews for advertising profiles.

To request access, correction or account deletion, contact our Discord support server. We verify identity before processing. Account deletion removes linked live reviews, posts, comments, votes and submission-limit records. Associated attachments are made unavailable and queued for Storage cleanup. We will explain any information retained for legal or safety reasons. Applicable rights depend on your location.

The site uses browser storage for sign-in sessions and the notice preference. See the storage policy. No advertising or analytics service has been added.

Posts, comments, attachments, voting and search

Published post titles, bodies, categories, comments, reply relationships, display names, opaque website author IDs, timestamps, moderation labels, Discord profile pictures, optional link labels and URLs, attachment filenames, descriptions, spoiler flags and files (including videos) are public. Spoiler blurring is a viewing preference, not an access restriction. Upload only information you intend to share publicly. Uploaded files may contain embedded metadata, such as location or author details; remove unwanted metadata before uploading.

We privately store which account voted on which post and its vote direction to enforce one vote per account. The public sees only the net score; a signed-in member can see their own vote. Moderation staff do not receive voter lists through the website. Backend operators can access these records when needed for security, operations or privacy requests.

We store account-linked submission times and limit posts, comments, file types, file sizes and total attachment slots to manage abuse and resources. Private moderation records contain actor, action, content reference and time, rather than copies of edited text. They are reviewed when privacy requests are received and retained only as needed for moderation accountability or legal obligations.

Posts, comments and votes remain until deleted by their owner, removed through moderation or deleted with the associated account. Post deletion also removes associated comments and votes. File deletion first removes public access, then requests deletion through Supabase Storage. Failed cleanup is retried on later signed-in visits and reviewed by the operator; abandoned drafts older than one day are removed on a later visit. Unlinked files are retained privately pending successful cleanup. Previously issued image links expire within five minutes; downloads, browser caches, external copies and provider backups may persist. Contact the operator for removal assistance.

The website navigation search matches page names entirely in your browser. The separate Community search sends post-search text and sorting or tag filters to Supabase. Search words can also appear in the community page URL and your browser history, so avoid entering private information. We do not create a search-history database or use searches for profiling; providers may retain ordinary operational request logs. No advertising or analytics trackers have been added.

Account agreement and consent records

We record your account identifier, declared account route (adult or guardian approval), policy version, server-recorded acceptance or withdrawal time, and acceptance history. These records are private and are not part of the public reviews feed. They remain while your account exists and are removed from the live database when the account is deleted. Provider backup retention still applies.

A guardian approval record contains the child’s Discord ID supplied after guardian consent, the policy version, consent and recording times, a reference to the privately held verification record, an approval-code hash, code expiry and any revocation time. Once linked to an account, the database approval record is removed with account deletion. Unused and withdrawn approvals and separately held verification correspondence are reviewed for removal when no longer needed for the approval, requests or a specific legal obligation. We do not publish guardian details or request identity documents through the website.

Account processing uses the choices shown before sign-in. A guardian’s own verified approval is required where applicable. We do not treat acknowledgement of this policy as consent to advertising or future payments.

Providers and international processing

Account, community content and attachment processing involves services outside Oman. The Supabase project database is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Discord, GitHub, jsDelivr and their service providers may process technical or account information in other countries under their own policies. Overseas legal protections may differ from those in Oman. The sign-in form requests a separate affirmative choice for the described overseas processing; guardian approval must cover it where required. Consent does not remove the operator’s responsibility to assess and apply the required transfer safeguards.

Information related to our Discord bots

When a member interacts with a custom bot, Discord may provide identifiers such as a user ID, server ID, channel ID, and details of the command or interaction. Depending on the bot feature used, user-submitted content such as support messages, applications, or attachments may also be involved. Not every bot processes every kind of information.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information through bot commands or support messages.

Why information may be used

Information associated with bot interactions may be used to carry out requested commands, respond to support messages, maintain community safety, prevent abuse, and resolve technical problems. We do not sell community member information or use this website for advertising profiles.

Storage and retention

Storage practices and retention depend on the specific bot feature and any moderation or support records that feature creates. We do not promise a universal deletion period. Members may contact us to ask what information is held about them, request deletion where appropriate, or raise concerns. Some records may need to be retained for safety, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Sharing and third parties

Relevant information may be accessible to authorized community staff when they handle moderation, support, or bot operations. Discord provides the platform on which the bots run. Technical hosting providers for the bots may also process information needed to operate them. Their identities and exact handling arrangements should be checked against each bot’s deployed configuration.

Young users and guardians

Website accounts require Discord’s minimum age for the member’s country. Members under 18, and others requiring a legal representative, use the Guardian Approval Process before sign-in. The guardian contacts the operator directly; the operator must verify their identity and authority and record explicit approval before issuing a code. The account route is a declaration, not independent age verification.

A guardian may request access, correction, withdrawal or deletion by contacting the operator. If we learn that an account lacks necessary approval, we restrict participation and review the information held. Do not send children’s personal details, passports, identity cards or birth certificates in public channels or through the website form.

Your choices and requests

For website accounts, Account & privacy → Withdraw account consent stops new reviews and edits immediately. Existing reviews remain public until removed; you can still delete your own reviews. Contact the operator to request account deletion, correction, access or withdrawal of guardian approval. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. We may retain limited information where a specific legal obligation requires it and will explain this when responding.

You can choose not to interact with community bots. To ask about bot data, request access or deletion, or report a privacy concern, email gta6onlinediscord@gmail.com. We may need enough detail to identify the relevant Discord interaction without asking you to disclose passwords or authentication tokens.

For the website’s small storage preference, see our Cookie & storage policyAccessibility.
