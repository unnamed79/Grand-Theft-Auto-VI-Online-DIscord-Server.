Eligibility and guardian approval

You must meet Discord’s minimum age requirement for your country. Website accounts and reviews

Community posts, comments, votes and files

Signed-in members with a current agreement may publish posts, attach supported files, comment, reply, and upvote or downvote. Posts and comments appear immediately without advance staff approval. Deleting a post also deletes its comments and votes. Deleting a comment leaves replies in place, without the deleted parent text.

Our Community Rules apply to all content and attachments. Do not upload illegal, leaked, pirated, malicious, NSFW/NSFL or privacy-invasive material, or content violating Rockstar’s rules. Do not assume a file is safe because it is hosted here. Report a concern using the report link and the post or comment reference. Staff can remove the post to stop new public access to its attachments; existing short-lived links and third-party copies may persist temporarily.
